# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and operators running autonomous AI agents on Solana. They open the
dashboard to monitor live deals, manage escrow positions, and track agent
activity. Technically sophisticated; not casual crypto users. They think in
transactions, state, and latency — not in UI affordances.

## Product Purpose

A private commerce layer for AI agents on Solana. Agents buy and sell services
through a TEE-backed escrow marketplace without revealing strategy, pricing, or
identity. Powered by MagicBlock's Private Ephemeral Rollups. Success means an
agent completes a deal from discovery through settlement with zero observable
metadata on-chain.

## Brand Personality

Secretive, precise, austere. The interface should feel like a classified ops
terminal: nothing decorative, everything deliberate. Quiet authority over
flashy confidence. The product's core promise is invisibility — the design
earns that by refusing ornament.

## Anti-references

- Neon crypto aesthetic (pump.fun, CoinGecko): no glow effects, no vivid
  color washes, no gradient-on-black hero treatments
- Friendly AI/SaaS (Vercel, Linear, Notion): Covert is not approachable by
  design. Warmth and onboarding-ease are not goals.
- Corporate fintech navy-and-gold: no institutional warmth, no trust badges,
  no reassurance patterns
- Glassmorphism as decoration: blur cards are cheap. If blur appears it must
  be functional (backdrop context, not aesthetics).

## Design Principles

1. Secrecy as aesthetic: the interface communicates restraint because the
   product is about concealment. Sparse > busy. Invisible > visible.
2. Precision over decoration: every element either carries data or controls
   action. Nothing exists purely to look good.
3. Agent-first legibility: information density is optimized for a human
   scanning agent state at a glance, not for onboarding a new user.
4. Monochrome as doctrine: color carries no decorative role. The one-accent
   rule is not a constraint — it is the point.
5. Tension through typography: hierarchy is expressed through scale, weight,
   and tracking contrast — not through color or illustration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. The
high-contrast monochrome palette benefits colorblind users by structural design,
not as an afterthought.
